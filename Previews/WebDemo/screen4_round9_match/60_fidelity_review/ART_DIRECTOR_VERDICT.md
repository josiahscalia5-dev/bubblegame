# Art director's verdict: `?s3=intunnel,fidelity` (7 November 2026, about 12:40 UTC, nineteenth session)

One independent review by `game-art-director` (read-only), the user's Option A. It looked only at the saved captures in
`../60_fidelity/` (sheets, 768-wide frames, Pixel 7 frames, ten stills from the comparison video); the game was not re-run.
**The scores are its visual judgement, not a pixel measurement.** The measured numbers are in `../60_fidelity/README.md`.

**Overall match to the ORIGINAL (out of 100): ACCEPTED 60, FIDELITY 64.**

| criterion (out of 10) | accepted | fidelity |
|---|---|---|
| 1. round, broad, fluffy dome | 4 | 7 |
| 2. blue/turquoise water, no pale film | 6 | 6 |
| 3. sits on top of the water | 7 | 7.5 |
| 4. rounded glassy lavender-blue mounds at her base | 2 | 5 |
| 5. natural blend of character and water | 4 | 4.5 |
| 6. motion (start, +1 s, swipes, jump, landing) | 6.5 | 5.5 |

- **Dome:** accepted is a narrow, peaked egg with a tuft; fidelity is clearly broader, but a little flat-topped and
  square-shouldered (a loaf), and wider and boxier from the front in the jump. The original is a smooth arc.
- **Water colour:** the same in both: blue, no white haze, but a paler, flatter periwinkle than the original's bright
  turquoise-and-white surf.
- **Height:** both sit on the water; fidelity shows slightly more of her (closer).
- **Mounds:** accepted has none (a toothed crest). Fidelity's are deep violet, smooth, soft-edged blobs in an even row
  across her whole base: they read as purple jelly beans or balloons. The original's are pale lavender-blue glass with
  crisp white rims, sitting in white foam and grouped mainly at her lower left.
- **Blend:** the hard toothed edge is gone and her pink shows through as violet (as in the original), but the row reads
  as a collar.
- **Motion:** the water flows past her in both, no flicker or popping; in fidelity the blobs ride along as separate
  objects (at the left swipe a large one sits on her flank like a stuck balloon).
- **NEW DEFECT in fidelity:** her model's dark gap between her legs (underside), mostly hidden by the crest in accepted,
  now shows above the water as a dark grey hole from the start to about 1.6 s (`art_director_hole_ACCEPTED_vs_FIDELITY_start.png`,
  `art_director_hole_video_frame.png`). Confirmed by the lead session.

**Verdict:** slightly closer to the original, not clearly closer. The broader crown is a real, visible win side by side;
the mounds point the right way but their colour and arrangement are wrong; on a phone in motion the gain is not obvious,
and the exposed hole is a new flaw.

**Biggest remaining differences, in order:** 1. the water in front of her (the original's bright frothy turquoise surf
with dense white foam and sculpted glassy mounds, against the game's pale flat periwinkle sheet with repeated white comma
strokes and rows of soap bubbles); 2. the contact mounds (pale glass with white rims, few and clustered, against violet
jelly in a row / none); 3. her fur and colour (the original's hot magenta, fine needle fur and glowing white edge against
a lighter bubblegum pink with chunky fur and pale square flecks; fidelity's crown flatter-topped).

**Recommendation: do not accept fidelity as it stands** (the hole; the violet blobs add a look the original does not
have; three changes bundled in one pass). The crown broadening is the one clear win: test it alone on the accepted game,
with the gap hidden or closed. Mounds, if tried again, as a separate step: two or three paler, glassier lavender-blue
domes with white rims, mainly at her lower left.

# What still differs from the ORIGINAL approved Screen 4 (6 November 2026, after step 7; no game change)

The user's question: "What looks different ... what is missing or different or not the same ... a list." Compared by eye,
zone by zone, the original (`Design/ReferencePacks/2026-11-04_Round6_Construction_Guide/01_Original_Approved_Screen_4.jpg`)
against the current game (`?s3=focus`, step 7, seed 7, 3.0 s after the tap, no steering, the page with the HUD), both at
767 x 1536: `01_top_...`, `02_tunnel_...`, `03_character_...` (original left, game right). Numbers from
`../35_step7_bubble_veil/measured.md` (masked water, `WebDemo/tools/screen4/layer_metrics.py`).

## Top: interface and frame
1. Behind the counters: the original is a glossy pale-lavender and blue shell; the game shows a flat purple drum ceiling
   with a dark seam and two small orange lights.
2. Counters: the original's are light glossy blue pills; the game's are dark navy, with heavier white numbers and a larger "+".
   The coin icon: a plain shiny gold coin in the original, a paw coin in the game.
3. Mission banner: light glossy aqua, glass-like, in the original; saturated royal blue in the game. Its sock icon: pink and
   white with a pink toe in the original; purple and white with a purple toe in the game.
4. Pause button: pale glossy blue in the original; bright blue with a white outline in the game.
5. The rim under the banner: a thick warm gold / bronze rim in the original; thinner and more lavender in the game. The
   original's blue glass orb at the right edge is missing.

## Tunnel
6. Colour: deep saturated cobalt and turquoise in the original; lighter, more pastel cyan in the game (walls 55.0 % against
   58.9 %, far centre 24.8 % against 29.7 %).
7. White splashes: many crisp, chunky, irregular white-aqua splashes, droplets and curls along every ring in the original; a
   few small procedural white dashes in the game (white pieces about 75 px against 237 px).
8. Rainbow: a broad, bright yellow-orange-pink arc round the upper middle in the original; softer, paler and more see-through
   in the game.
9. Distant centre: a warm golden-lit core with crisp blue and white rings and small far objects (a little creature and a
   striped sock) in the original; paler and milkier in the game, with softer pastel rings.
10. Lighting and depth: bright, glowing, sparkling depth in the original; softer and flatter in the game.
11. Bubbles: the original's big bubble at the upper right has a pink-purple creature inside, and its bubbles have glossy white
    highlights with a lavender tint; the game's are empty thin clear outlines.

## Objects
12. Coins: warmer orange-gold with a soft glow and thick rims in the original; flatter metallic yellow in the game. The far
    centre coin is a shiny plain ring in the original, a paw coin in the game.
13. Gem: about the same; the game's a little smaller and darker violet.
14. Sock: white with lavender stripes, a BLUE toe, lying sideways, eyes closed in a happy smile in the original; purple stripes,
    a purple fuzzy toe, more upright, open eyes with pupils and blush in the game.
15. Brushes: soft yarn-like pink and white (one with a blue core), cropped at the left and right edges, in the original; spikier
    and rounder in the game, and from about 5.5 s of the ride they grow large and fill the middle of the screen.

## Character and water
16. The pink character: rounder, deeper hot pink with a soft glow and side tufts in the original; lighter pink, spikier and
    flatter in the game (postponed by the user).
17. Water round her: big frothy white-blue waves with bright white crests and lavender-tinted foam bubbles in the original; blue
    water with white curved streaks, less froth, flatter in the game.
18. The floor ahead: bright turquoise with white foam lines and sparkles in the original; paler light blue in the game.

## Bottom: power-up buttons
19. In the original they are square glossy buttons cut off by the bottom edge, with no text labels: a pink bubble-blaster with
    bubbles and a yellow propeller. In the game they are whole rounded buttons, higher up, labelled "Bubble Blast" and
    "Turbo Spin", with a pink hammer-like blaster and a yellow flower.

# Roller candidates No. 1 and No. 2: evidence (3 November 2026)

All from the running browser game (headless Chromium, software WebGL) or from three.js renders of the delivered GLBs. None of it is a mockup. Write-up and recommendation: [`Design/SCREEN3_ROLLER_CANDIDATES.md`](../../../Design/SCREEN3_ROLLER_CANDIDATES.md).

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `01_opening_candidates.png` | Approved Screen 3, the test version, and the test version with No. 1 and with No. 2 in the right-hand slot. Same camera and moment (Tap to Start), 538 × 1076. |
| `02_right_slot_closeups.png` | The right-hand slot of those four frames, enlarged. |
| `03_models_inspected.png` | Both GLBs from six views, and No. 1 with each separate piece coloured (the −Z bracket plate is welded to the drum). |
| `04_spin_test.png` | What spinning would do: No. 1's welded bracket plate and No. 2's gold bars turn with the drum. Neither is spun in the test. |
| `05_left_slot_trial.png` | No. 1 tried in place of the Meshy side machinery: too small and pink beside the approved blue-faced brush. |
| `roller1_gameplay.mp4` | 13 s of real gameplay with No. 1 in the opening: Tap to Start, swipes, into the same tunnel, socks and coins collected, loop. |
| `roller1_journey.png` | Twelve frames from that recording, Tap to Start to inside the tunnel. |

Re-create with `?roller=1` or `?roller=2` on the page (also the last argument of `tools/start_frame.cjs`), or `ROLLER=1` before `tools/record.cjs`, `tools/trace.cjs` or `tools/seeded_shots.cjs`.

## 01_WATER_LOOK_gold_rim_glossy_turquoise_floor.png (941 x 1672)

The tunnel as a body of water.

- **Floor:** a glossy, saturated turquoise floor runs from the foreground into the centre.
  - It is covered in rippling wave facets.
  - A bright path of sun glints runs straight down the middle toward the glowing centre: specular reflection of the light
    at the end of the tunnel.
- **Walls:** the walls are swirling rings of water, cobalt in the shadows and cyan where lit, with sparkles.
- **Rainbow rings:** pastel rings (peach, pink, lilac) sit round a warm white-gold centre.
- **Architecture:** a lavender housing and a polished gold rim at the top.
- **Foam:** white foam banks lie only at the sides and bottom, with clear soap bubbles. The blue water is plainly visible
  between them; the foam does not cover the floor.

## 02_WATER_LOOK_electric_blue_vortex_white_splash_strokes.png (941 x 1672)

The vortex's motion and colour.

- **Colour:** deep electric blue to cyan, with glossy highlights along the spiral bands.
- **Foam:** white splash strokes, broken and irregular with droplets flying off, lie along the spirals as accents on the
  blue, not as a layer.
- **Detail:** sparkles and clear bubbles.
- **Foreground:** splash crests and foam at the bottom edge.
